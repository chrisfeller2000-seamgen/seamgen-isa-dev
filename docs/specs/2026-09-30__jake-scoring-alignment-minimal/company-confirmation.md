# Company facts needing confirmation

The archive is suitable for reviewing historical delivery. It is not a current eligibility certificate. No claim below has been newly verified with a company owner, and no original company record was rewritten.

Before relying on a fact for a mandatory RFP requirement, obtain dated supporting records from the responsible person. Only confirm facts relevant to that opportunity; a general company audit is not a prerequisite for every score.

| Fact | Evidence needed before an unconditional pass |
| --- | --- |
| Government and commercial references | Which named projects qualify under the buyer's wording, whether required work is completed, and whether the references are currently usable. Government customers of a commercial client do not make that client a government reference. |
| Required registrations or set-asides | Current company registration/status and any applicable validity period. A historical proposal or the rubric's qualifications list is insufficient. |
| Required certificates | Exact certificate, named company or individual holder as appropriate, date/status, and applicability to the requirement. Delivery experience and a cloud provider's compliance do not prove a held company credential. |
| Partner-dependent eligibility | Named partner, confirmed willingness/role, applicable qualifications/references, and whether it sells through integrators when that is the proposed route. A hypothetical partner does not qualify. |
| Specific technical operations | Named engineer's dated confirmation of the actual operation and project. Keep exact delivery, adjacent experience, and unanswered questions separate. |
| Submission access | A confirmed allowed method, existing account, or registration feasible before the deadline. A buyer's contact email is not an accepted submission method. |

Store approved private evidence outside Git in the existing company files or a separately reviewed source-loading path. Do not invent an attestation or silently append one based on a model answer. The current loader reads Case Studies and Assets, not People or an arbitrary new qualifications file.

Until supporting evidence is supplied, leave the relevant eligibility fact unknown. A clear independent gate failure can still justify No-bid; an unresolved mandatory requirement cannot justify an unconditional pursue result.
